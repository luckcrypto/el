    Is Elon Musk still a trillionaire?

    Yes.

    The latest Forbes reading in this record is $1.051 trillion for 6 October 2026, which is above $1 trillion. Forbes says he first passed that mark on 12 June 2026, the day SpaceX began trading.

    Year by year since 1999

    From the $22 million he made selling Zip2 to $1.051 trillion in the latest reading: every year with a published figure, on a scale where each line up is ten times the one below.

    1999 and 2002 are the money he took from selling Zip2 and PayPal, not a full net worth. From 2012 each figure is his entry on the Forbes World’s Billionaires list, published every March and measured a few weeks before. Nothing reliable was published for the years between.

    What a reading is

    A reading is the figure Forbes published at the moment we looked, shown to the precision the source gave. Forbes revises its estimate through the trading day, so two readings on the same date can differ. We never fill the gaps between readings or draw a line through them.
