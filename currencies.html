    In rupees

    In Indian rupees the figure is ₹10,12,08,46,70,65,334, or ₹101.2 lakh crore, at 96.30 rupees to the dollar on 5 October 2026.

    How the conversion is done

    Each figure is the Forbes estimate in US dollars multiplied by one reference rate. Euro rates come from the European Central Bank, and the other currencies are crossed through the dollar from the same table. The dirham is pegged at 3.6725 to the dollar by the Central Bank of the UAE. Rates are stamped when we take a reading and do not move in between, so a figure here can differ from one worked out with today's market rate.

    Why a converted figure moves on its own

    Two things move it: the dollar estimate and the exchange rate. A weaker rupee raises the rupee figure even when the dollar estimate has not changed.
