    How rich is Elon Musk?

    The answers below explain what a number like that does and does not mean.

    How much is Elon Musk worth today?

    The figure at the top of this page is the most recent Forbes estimate we have checked, shown with the date Forbes gave it and the date we read it. Calculate now swaps it for our estimate, the same reading moved by Tesla and SpaceX share prices since, and labels it as ours.

    Is net worth the same as money in the bank?

    No. Net worth is the estimated value of what someone owns, less what they owe. Most of this figure is company shares. Selling on that scale would move the price and trigger tax, and part of his Tesla holding is restricted until January 2028.

    How do Tesla and SpaceX affect his wealth?

    Both are listed, so the value of his shares moves with each share price during market hours. A company's market value is the worth of the whole company, not of his part of it.

    Read the Tesla stake note

    Why do net worth estimates disagree?

    Publishers use different share counts, different dates, different treatment of options and debt, and different valuations for private companies. Printing a figure to the last dollar removes none of that.

    Where the prices come from

    Your browser asks Robinhood for Tesla and SpaceX prices, which it quotes through the night on weekdays. If that fails it asks CoinGecko for tokens that track the two shares and trade at any hour, and labels them as token prices. If neither answers, it uses the latest close on our record. Every figure says which one it used and when.

    Which shares are counted

    Tesla: the 413,152,109 shares in his trust. The 286,428,773 restricted shares from his 2018 options are left out, as Forbes leaves them out, and so is the 2025 award, which has not vested.

    SpaceX: the 4,766,475,230 shares his trusts hold and his 350,000,000 options, which gain or lose a dollar for every dollar the share moves. The 1,302,072,285 restricted shares that have not vested are left out. Both counts come from his SEC filings.
