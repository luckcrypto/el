    The publication

    What this project does

    Elon Musk Net Worth is an independent, unofficial publication. We distinguish dated publisher observations from our own market-adjusted calculations. Neither is a personal bank balance.

    Who is responsible

    Published by Luck (luck.fyi). Corrections and enquiries: business@luck.fyi. The figures here are not audited and no expert review is claimed.

    Our editorial standard

    • Use dated primary evidence for ownership, transactions and liabilities.
    • Preserve source observation dates; a page refresh is not a new observation.
    • Show uncertainty and unavailable inputs instead of filling gaps with invented figures.
    • Reconcile parent and subsidiary interests before adding values together.
    • Document material changes through our corrections record.

    Commercial independence

    Merchandise and optional support do not buy influence over calculations. Any paid placement must be labelled Sponsored. Our .org domain does not indicate charitable registration or tax-deductible donations.

    Entertainment

    The armoured companion and the landing game are fictional entertainment. Companion dialogue is original parody, not a quotation or endorsement by Elon Musk.
