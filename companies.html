    Where sources differ

    His stakes are reported in more than one way: with or without options, as a share of the equity or as a share of the vote, and on different dates. Where two sources we accept give different figures, both are printed here with their dates. We do not pick one.

    • Tesla, his stake: 19.9% of its common stock, counting 413,152,109 shares in his trust and 286,428,773 restricted shares that carry his vote, and leaving out the 2025 performance award (His Schedule 13G/A for Tesla, SEC, 17 June 2026); About 12%, excluding options (Forbes guide to his fortune, 22 November 2024).
    • SpaceX, his stake: 48.4% of Class A on an as-converted basis, counting restricted shares and options (His Schedule 13G for SpaceX, SEC, 30 June 2026); 38%, including options (Forbes profile of Elon Musk, 5 October 2026); 42% (Forbes on the Tesla share rout, 11 June 2026).
    • SpaceX, voting power after the offering: About 83.6% (SpaceX EU prospectus, 5 June 2026); 84.4% (SpaceX prospectus filed with the SEC, 1 May 2026).

    How the board is checked

    Filings and company statements come first, then Forbes. Wikipedia and round-up sites are used to find a source, never as the source. A fact we cannot trace is left off. The board is re-read with each new reading.
